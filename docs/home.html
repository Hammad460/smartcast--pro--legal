SmartCast Pro

Your iPhone is your Android TV remote.

Control your TV, cast photos and videos, mirror your screen, and keep private files in a protected vault.

Platform iPhone, available on the App Store.
Works with Android TV and Chromecast-enabled devices.
Account No account required.

SmartCast Pro is a utility app that connects your iPhone to your TV over your Wi-Fi network.

Download SmartCast Pro on the App Store

Features

  • Android TV Remote: navigation, volume, power, channel controls, touchpad mode, number pad, quick apps, and voice search.
  • Photo and Video Casting: choose media from your photo library and cast it to your TV over your local network.
  • Screen Mirroring: mirror your iPhone screen to a compatible receiver using the SmartCast broadcast extension.
  • Private Vault: store selected photos, videos, and files inside a private area in the app.

Privacy

We do not sell your personal information, and private vault content is stored on your device. Read the full Privacy Policy for details.

Support

For help with setup, casting, mirroring, or subscriptions, visit the support page or email smartcastprosupport@gmail.com.
